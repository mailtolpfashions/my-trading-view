TradingView-style samples

All three use the familiar TradingView workspace. They differ in what surrounds the chart. Everything is clickable; prices are sample data.

1 · Classic

Pure TradingView feel: drawing tools on the left, toolbar on top, OHLC legend with BUY/SELL, watermark, RSI pane, range bar, watchlist + symbol details on the right.

Try: Indicators → add Bollinger / VWAP · chart type → Heikin Ashi · trend-line tool → click two points · 5m/15m/1h/1D · click watchlist rows.

 2 · F&O Trader

Same chart plus an option chain panel (OI bars, PCR, max pain), paper-trading order ticket, positions/orders/signal log at the bottom, and a Signals panel with published rules and backtest stats.

Try: hover a CE/PE price → B or S → place a paper order and watch P&L tick · rail icons switch Watchlist / Option chain / Signals.

 3 · Multi-chart terminal

2×2 layout (NIFTY, BANKNIFTY, NIFTY futures, ATM straddle) with synced crosshair, plus an OI build-up scanner, market breadth and FII/DII flows.

Try: move the mouse over one chart — the others follow · click a chart to make it active, then change its timeframe.
